# Referee findings

## Central mathematical assessment

No mathematical defect was identified in the central reduction or finite
certificate. The result covers arbitrary complex, nonnormal, non-Hermitian
27 by 27 matrices of rank at most two, not merely a restricted support family.
The physical lifting permits unrestricted B, including rank-one input cases.
The real averaging is applied to moment tuples, not as a restriction on C.
The coupled cross block is the literal nonsymmetrised Gram cross term.
Positive local metrics come from actual physical rational embeddings.
The selected 272 equality rows need only be valid; no claim that they span all
2,912 raw rows is needed. Likewise completeness of the zero families used in
discovery is not required once the final global identity is verified.

## Editorial recommendation

Major revisions, directed at scholarly positioning and the scope of ancillary
reproducibility claims, not at replacing the main theorem or running more
numerical witness searches. A competent specialist external review remains
appropriate before publication of a major claim.

## Major revisions

1. The main proof has no conventional related-work section or bibliography.
   Relevant citations occur in the nested historical input note, but must be
   promoted into the main article. Compare Costa Rico (2025), the July 2026
   two-copy papers, the maximally mixed auxiliary-qubit formulation, Wu and
   Zou's three-copy restricted-sector work, and earlier semidefinite witness
   methods. Separate inherited reductions from the explicit new certificate.
   A bounded search did not locate an earlier proof of the same unrestricted
   qutrit three-copy endpoint; this is not an exhaustive priority certificate.

2. Separate final-theorem replay from historical construction claims. The
   optional relative correction bound in proof.md equation (28a) is supported
   by a receipt referring to historical work/ paths, rather than by baseline
   data that the portable replay reconstructs. Either ship those inputs and
   a check, or remove/relabel that ancillary statement. The endpoint theorem
   does not rely on it: all final corrected Grams and the entire functional
   identity were checked afresh.

## Minor and strengthening points

The ru_maxrss field is labelled bytes without platform conversion; Linux and
macOS use different units. The existing resource supervisor's aggregate-memory
record is separate. Clarify the platform scope of resource/SIGALRM/hard-link
use. The frozen certificate's PENDING_FRESH_REPLAY metadata is historical,
not a failed current status; a clearer construction-status label would help
in the next release without changing the already frozen archive. Give the
article a date/version and a publication-facing entry point, maintain a clear
separation of original inputs from the final result, and specify reuse terms.

The full three-copy qutrit parameter threshold is an immediate corollary;
see parameter_corollary.md. It strengthens the result's presentation without
requiring a new certificate, a four-copy theorem or a dimension-free theorem.
